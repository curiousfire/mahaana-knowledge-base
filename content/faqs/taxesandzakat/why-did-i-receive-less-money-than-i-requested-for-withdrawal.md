question: Why did I receive less money than I requested for withdrawal?
answer: >
  There are two possible reasons.

  Capital Gains Tax. A 15% tax is deducted automatically, but only from the
  profit portion of your withdrawal. For example, if you withdraw PKR 10,000 and
  PKR 1,000 of that is profit, 15% tax (PKR 150) is deducted from the profit
  only, so you receive PKR 9,850. This is a tax requirement, not a hidden fee or
  bank charge, and it is remitted directly to the government.

  Early exit charge. From 12 October 2026, money withdrawn within 30 days of
  being deposited carries a 1% early exit charge. Money deposited before 12
  October, or held for 30 days or more, is not charged.
