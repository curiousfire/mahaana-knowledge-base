question: Are there hidden fees when I withdraw my money? I requested funds but received less.
answer: >
  There are no hidden fees on withdrawals. There are two reasons you can receive
  less than you requested.

  Capital Gains Tax. A mandatory 15% tax applies only to the profit portion of
  your withdrawal. It is required by law and is calculated and deducted
  automatically at the time of withdrawal. The rate is the same for tax filers
  and non-filers, and your account statement shows exactly how much was
  deducted.

  Early exit charge. From 12 October 2026, money withdrawn within 30 days of
  being deposited carries a 1% early exit charge. Money held for 30 days or more
  is not charged.
