question: "Is this like a bank account? Can I take my money out anytime?"
answer: >
  While it's not a bank account, it's just as accessible. You can withdraw your
  money any time, typically within 1-2 business days, and there is no lock in.
  The main difference is you have the potential to earn better returns than a
  regular bank account.

  One thing to know. From 12 October 2026, money withdrawn within 30 days of
  being deposited carries a 1% early exit charge. After 30 days, withdrawals
  are free.
