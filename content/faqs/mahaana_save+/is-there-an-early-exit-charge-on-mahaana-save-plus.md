---
question: Is there an early exit charge on Mahaana Save+?
answer: >
  Yes, from Monday 12 October 2026. Money withdrawn within 30 days of being
  deposited carries a 1% early exit charge. After 30 days, withdrawals stay
  free.

  Each deposit has its own 30 day clock, and withdrawals always come from your
  oldest deposits first, so money you have held for a month or more is never
  charged.
