question: How much will I pay if I withdraw within 30 days?
answer: >
  1.5% of the part of your withdrawal that comes from deposits less than 30 days
  old. The rest of your withdrawal is free.

  For example, you deposit Rs 50,000 on 1 November and Rs 20,000 on 20 November.
  On 5 December you withdraw Rs 60,000.

  Your oldest money comes out first. The first Rs 50,000 comes from your 1
  November deposit, which is 34 days old, so it is free. The remaining Rs 10,000
  comes from your 20 November deposit, which is only 15 days old, so 1.5% applies
  to it. Your early exit charge is Rs 150.
