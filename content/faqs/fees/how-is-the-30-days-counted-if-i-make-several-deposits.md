---
question: How is the 30 days counted if I make several deposits?
answer: >
  Every deposit has its own 30 day clock, starting on the day you made that
  deposit. A new deposit does not restart the clock on your older money.

  When you withdraw, money always comes out of your oldest deposits first. So
  money you have held for 30 days or more is used up before any newer money is
  touched, and it is never charged.
