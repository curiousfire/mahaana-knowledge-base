question: Does the early exit charge apply to money I deposited before 12 October 2026?
answer: >
  No. Any money you deposited before Monday 12 October 2026 is exempt, however
  soon you withdraw it. The charge only applies to deposits made on or after 12
  October.

  For example, you deposited Rs 20,000 on 5 October and Rs 10,000 on 14 October.
  On 20 October you withdraw Rs 25,000. Your oldest money comes out first, so
  the first Rs 20,000 comes from your 5 October deposit and is exempt. The
  remaining Rs 5,000 comes from your 14 October deposit, which is only 6 days
  old, so 1% applies to it. Your early exit charge is Rs 50.
