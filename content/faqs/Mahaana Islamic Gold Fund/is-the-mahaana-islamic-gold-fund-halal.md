question: Is the Mahaana Islamic Gold Fund halal?
answer: >
  Yes. The fund is set up as Wakalatul Istithmar, which means you are the owner
  and Mahaana is your appointed agent. It holds real gold, never borrows, never
  short sells, and never sells gold it does not own. Al Hilal Shariah Advisors
  vet the fund, and a shariah review is carried out every year and published in
  the annual report.
