question: Why is my Gold Fund investment showing as pending or verification in progress?
answer: >
  A new investment takes one to two working days to settle before your units are
  issued. During that time it shows as pending in the app. Once settled, the
  pending status clears and your holding appears.

  This is normal processing time and has nothing to do with the IPO. If it has
  been longer than two working days, contact support and we will check it for
  you.
