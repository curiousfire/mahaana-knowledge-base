---
question: How do I see what my gold is worth?
answer: >
  Open the app. It shows the current value of your holding, what you put in, and
  your profit or loss. The app does not display the NAV figure itself.

  Your value updates once per working day, after the NAV is struck at market
  close and applied the same day by 10.30pm. It does not move continuously
  through the day.
