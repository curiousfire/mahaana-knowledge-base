---
question: Why has my gold value not moved today?
answer: >
  Your value updates once per working day, after the NAV is applied by 10.30pm
  that night. So if you check during the day, you are still looking at the
  previous working day's figure.

  If the gold price barely moved that day, your profit or loss will barely move
  either. On weekends and public holidays no price is struck at all, so your
  value stays where it was at the last close.
