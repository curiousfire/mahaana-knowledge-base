question: What does it cost to invest in the Gold Fund during the IPO?
answer: >
  One charge. The 1% PMEX charge applies to your investment.

  The 1.5% front end load is waived in full for the IPO. That is the launch
  offer, and it is the only charge being waived.

  The PMEX charge is not waived during the IPO. It is not waived at any time,
  for anyone. It is not a Mahaana fee. It is paid to Pakistan Mercantile
  Exchange to carry out the gold trade, and you would pay it on any gold trade
  on that exchange whether you went through this fund or traded there yourself.
