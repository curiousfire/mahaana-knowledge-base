---
question: What does it cost to invest in the Gold Fund during the IPO?
answer: >
  Nothing on the way in. The front end charge is waived for the launch, and
  because units are issued at Rs 10, there is no PMEX charge added to your price
  either. The fund pays the market's buying spread once, when it buys the gold
  after the window closes, and that is already reflected in the first price you
  see.
