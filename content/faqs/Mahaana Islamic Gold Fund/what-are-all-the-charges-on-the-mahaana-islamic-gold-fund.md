question: What are all the charges on the Mahaana Islamic Gold Fund?
answer: >
  There are four, and this is the complete list.

  Front end load, 1.5% of what you invest. Waived in full during the IPO. It
  applies to every investment made after the IPO closes.

  PMEX charge, about 1%. Charged on every deposit and on every withdrawal. It is
  never waived. It is charged during the IPO as well.

  Back end load, 1.5%. Charged only if you withdraw a deposit within 30 days of
  making that deposit. After 30 days that deposit carries no back end load.

  Management fee, 1.5% a year, plus the fund's running costs. Taken from the
  fund's value a little each day, so the price you see in the app is already net
  of it.

  Nothing else is charged. There is no account fee, no custody fee billed to you
  separately, and no charge for holding.
