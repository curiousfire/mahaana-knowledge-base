question: What does the Mahaana Islamic Gold Fund cost while I hold it?
answer: >
  The fund charges 1.5% a year for managing the gold, plus its running costs
  such as vault custody, takaful cover, and trustee and exchange charges. These
  come out of the fund's value a little each day, so the price you see in the
  app is already net of them. The fund's total yearly cost is published every
  month in the fund manager report.
