question: Who is holding my gold in the Mahaana Islamic Gold Fund?
answer: >
  Central Depository Company of Pakistan is the Trustee. The fund's bank
  accounts are in CDC's name as trustee, and the gold sits in vault custody with
  takaful cover paid for by the fund. None of it sits on Mahaana's books.
  Mahaana manages it. CDC holds it. You own it.
