question: Do I pay tax on my Mahaana Islamic Gold Fund gains?
answer: >
  Yes. Capital Gains Tax of 15% applies, and it applies to your gain only. It is
  never charged on the money you put in. This is exactly the same treatment as
  Mahaana Save+ and the other Mahaana funds.

  It is calculated and deducted automatically at the time of withdrawal, on the
  profit portion of that withdrawal. The rate is the same whether you are a tax
  filer or not.

  For example, if you withdraw Rs 10,000 and Rs 1,000 of that is gain, 15% of
  the Rs 1,000 is deducted, so Rs 150 in tax, and you receive Rs 9,850. If gold
  has fallen and you have no gain, there is no Capital Gains Tax to pay.

  This is a government tax, not a Mahaana charge. It is remitted directly to the
  government, and it is separate from the fund's charges. Your annual gain and
  loss report, which covers PMEX along with your other investments, is available
  from the NCConnect app by NCCPL.
