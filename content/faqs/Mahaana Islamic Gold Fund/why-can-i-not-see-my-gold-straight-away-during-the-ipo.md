---
question: Why can I not see my gold straight away during the IPO?
answer: >
  Because the fund has not bought it yet. During the window your investment
  shows as pending in the app, and your money is held in the fund's account, not
  invested. After the window closes at 4pm on Thursday 10 September, the fund
  goes to PMEX and buys gold for everyone at once. Your units are then issued at
  Rs 10, the pending status clears, and from Friday 11 September your app shows
  what your gold is worth every day.
