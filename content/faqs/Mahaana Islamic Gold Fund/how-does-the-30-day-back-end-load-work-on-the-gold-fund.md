question: How does the 30 day back end load work if I have made several deposits?
answer: >
  Every deposit has its own 30 day clock, running from the day you made that
  deposit. The clock is not reset by later deposits and it is not shared between
  them.

  So if you invested in March and again in July, the March money passed its 30
  days long ago and can be withdrawn with no back end load. The July money
  carries the 1.5% back end load until its own 30 days are up.

  The 1.5% is charged only on the portion you withdraw that is still inside its
  30 day window, not on your whole holding.
