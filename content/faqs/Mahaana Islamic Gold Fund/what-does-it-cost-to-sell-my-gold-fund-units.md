question: What does it cost to take money out of the Mahaana Islamic Gold Fund?
answer: >
  The 1% PMEX charge applies to every withdrawal, always. Selling gold on the
  exchange costs the same as buying it, so the charge applies on the way out
  exactly as it did on the way in.

  On top of that, a back end load of 1.5% applies only if you withdraw a deposit
  within 30 days of making that deposit. If the deposit you are withdrawing is
  older than 30 days, there is no back end load on it.

  So a withdrawal of money that has been in the fund for more than 30 days costs
  you the 1% PMEX charge and nothing else.
