question: What does it cost to invest in the Gold Fund?
answer: >
  There are four charges and this is the complete list.

  Front end load. Up to 1.5% of what you invest, charged when you buy.

  PMEX charge. About 1%, charged when you buy and again when you sell. This is
  not a fee Mahaana keeps. It is the gap between the price gold can be bought at
  and the price it can be sold at on PMEX. Anyone buying gold anywhere pays this
  gap, including at a jeweller. Here it is shown to you openly instead of being
  hidden in the price.

  Back end load. 1.5%, charged only if you withdraw a deposit within 30 days of
  making that deposit. After 30 days that deposit carries no back end load.

  Management fee. 1.5% a year plus the fund's running costs. This is taken from
  the fund's value a little each day, so it is already reflected in the profit
  or loss you see. You are never billed for it separately.

  Nothing else is charged by Mahaana. Separately, Capital Gains Tax of 15%
  applies on your gain when you withdraw. That is a government tax, not a
  Mahaana charge.
