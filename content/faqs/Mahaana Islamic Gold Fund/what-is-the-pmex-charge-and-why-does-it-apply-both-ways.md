question: What is the PMEX charge, and why does it apply on the way in and on the way out?
answer: >
  It is about 1%, and it is charged on every deposit and every withdrawal,
  including during the IPO. It is never waived.

  Mahaana does not keep it. It is paid to Pakistan Mercantile Exchange to carry
  out the gold trade. Buying gold on the exchange has a cost and selling it has
  a cost, which is why the charge applies in both directions.

  If you opened your own account and traded gold on PMEX directly, you would pay
  this same charge yourself. Going through the fund does not add it and does not
  avoid it. The difference is that here it is shown to you plainly instead of
  being buried in the price.
