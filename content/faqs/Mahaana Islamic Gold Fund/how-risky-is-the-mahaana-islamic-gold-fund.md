question: How risky is the Mahaana Islamic Gold Fund?
answer: >
  It is a high risk fund. Gold prices go down as well as up, sometimes sharply,
  sometimes for years at a time. The fund stays in gold whether the price is
  rising or falling and does not protect itself against a fall. Your capital is
  at risk and you may get back less than you put in. This is the right product
  if you already want to own gold for the long run. It is the wrong product for
  money you may need soon.
