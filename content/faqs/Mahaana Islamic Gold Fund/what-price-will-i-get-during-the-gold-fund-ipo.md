---
question: What price will I get during the Gold Fund IPO?
answer: >
  Rs 10 a unit. Everyone who invests during the window gets the same price,
  whether you come in on the first day or the last.

  The 1% PMEX charge applies to your investment, so the number of units you
  receive is worked out after that charge is applied. The unit price itself
  stays at Rs 10 for the whole window.

  The price does not move during the window, because the fund has not bought any
  gold yet. From Friday 11 September, once the gold is bought, your unit price
  starts moving with the price of gold, up and down, every working day.
