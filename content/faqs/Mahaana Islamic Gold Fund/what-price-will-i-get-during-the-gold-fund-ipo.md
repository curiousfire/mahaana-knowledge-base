---
question: What price will I get during the Gold Fund IPO?
answer: >
  Rs 10 a unit. Everyone who invests in the window gets the same price, whether
  you come in on Friday or the following Thursday. Rs 10,000 buys you 1,000
  units, Rs 50,000 buys you 5,000 units.

  The price does not move during the window, because the fund has not bought any
  gold yet. From Friday 11 September, once the gold is bought, your unit price
  starts moving with the price of gold, up and down, every working day.
