question: Is the Mahaana Islamic Gold Fund actually gold, or is it paper that follows the gold price?
answer: >
  It is gold. The fund can only hold contracts that are backed by physical gold,
  and almost all of the fund sits in that gold. A small amount stays in cash at
  Islamic banks so the fund can pay people who want to sell on any given day.
  The fund is not allowed to borrow, short sell, or sell gold it does not
  already own.
