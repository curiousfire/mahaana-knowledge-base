question: What is the Mahaana Islamic Gold Fund?
answer: >
  It is a way to own real gold from your phone. You put in rupees. The fund uses
  that money to buy deliverable gold on PMEX, Pakistan's regulated commodity
  exchange. That gold sits in a vault, held in trust for the people who
  invested. You hold units, and every unit is your share of all the gold the
  fund owns.
