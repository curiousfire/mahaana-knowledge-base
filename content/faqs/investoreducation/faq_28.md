---
question: "What if I need my money urgently?"
answer: >
  You can request a withdrawal any time through your online account. It
  typically takes 1-2 business days to reach your bank account. There is no
  lock in.

  One thing to know. From 12 October 2026, money withdrawn within 30 days of
  being deposited carries a 1% early exit charge. Money you have held for 30
  days or more is withdrawn free.
---
