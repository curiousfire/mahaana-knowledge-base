## First, what is this

### What is the Mahaana Islamic Gold Fund?

It is a way to own real gold from your phone. You put in rupees. The fund uses that money to buy deliverable gold on PMEX, Pakistan's regulated commodity exchange. That gold sits in a vault, held in trust for the people who invested. You hold units, and every unit is your share of all the gold the fund owns.

### Is it actually gold, or is it paper that follows the gold price?

It is gold. The fund can only hold contracts that are backed by physical gold, and almost all of the fund sits in that gold. A small amount, around 2 to 5%, stays in cash at Islamic banks so the fund can pay people who want to sell on any given day. The fund is not allowed to borrow, short sell, or sell gold it does not already own.

### Why would I use this instead of buying gold from a jeweller?

Both give you gold. This one gives it to you without the things that usually come with it. There is no locker to rent, no making charge, no purity to verify, and no buyer to find when you want to sell. You buy and sell in rupees, in the app, on any working day.

### Is it halal?

Yes. The fund is set up as Wakalatul Istithmar, which means you are the owner and Mahaana is your appointed agent. It holds real gold, never borrows, never short sells, and never sells gold it does not own. Al Hilal Shariah Advisors vet the fund, and a shariah review is carried out every year and published in the annual report.

### How risky is it?

It is a high risk fund. Gold prices go down as well as up, sometimes sharply, sometimes for years at a time. The fund stays in gold whether the price is rising or falling and does not protect itself against a fall. Your capital is at risk and you may get back less than you put in. This is the right product if you already want to own gold for the long run. It is the wrong product for money you may need soon.

## The launch

### I keep seeing the word IPO. What does that mean for a fund?

IPO stands for initial public offering. It is the first window in which the fund takes in money from the public. Everyone who invests during the window gets in at the same starting price, and the fund buys its first gold with the money collected. After the window closes, the fund opens for normal daily buying and selling.

### When does the IPO open and close?

It opens at 4pm on Friday 4 September 2026 and closes at 4pm on Thursday 10 September 2026. Investments placed in the app between those two times are part of the IPO.

### What happens if I invest after 4pm on Thursday 10 September?

Your investment is not part of the IPO. It is treated as a normal purchase once the fund opens for daily dealing, at that day's price, with normal charges.

### Why is there a launch window at all?

A fund needs a starting pool before it can buy its first gold. The window brings everyone in together so the fund can go to PMEX with one order, at one price, and start clean. It also lets us waive the front end charge for everyone who joins in the first week.

### How much is the fund trying to raise?

Rs 100 million. That is the minimum size the SECP requires for a fund of this kind to operate.

## Putting money in

### How much do I need to start?

Rs 10,000 for your first investment. After that, every further investment is Rs 5,000 or more.

### How do I invest?

Open the Mahaana app, choose the Gold Fund, enter the amount, and pay from your own bank account. If you do not have a Mahaana account yet, you can open one in the app with your CNIC and a selfie. It takes about ten minutes.

### What price will I get during the IPO?

Rs 10 a unit. Everyone who invests in the window gets the same price, whether you come in on Friday or the following Thursday. Rs 10,000 buys you 1,000 units, Rs 50,000 buys you 5,000 units.

The price does not move during the window, because the fund has not bought any gold yet. From Friday 11 September, once the gold is bought, your unit price starts moving with the price of gold, up and down, every working day.

### Why can I not see my gold straight away?

Because the fund has not bought it yet. During the window your investment shows as pending in the app, and your money is held in the fund's account, not invested. After the window closes at 4pm on Thursday 10 September, the fund goes to PMEX and buys gold for everyone at once. Your units are then issued at Rs 10, the pending status clears, and from Friday 11 September your app shows what your gold is worth every day.

### So my money just sits there for a week?

During the window, yes. It is held in the fund's account with the Trustee, not with Mahaana, and it is not put into gold until the window closes. That is how every investor gets the same starting price.

### Can I change my mind during the window?

Once your investment is placed in the IPO it is committed to the launch. You can sell your units on any working day after the fund opens for daily dealing.

## What it costs

### What does it cost to invest during the IPO?

Nothing on the way in. The front end charge is waived for the launch, and because units are issued at Rs 10, there is no PMEX charge added to your price either. The fund pays the market's buying spread once, when it buys the gold after the window closes, and that is already reflected in the first price you see.

### What does it cost to invest after the IPO?

Once the fund is open for daily dealing, buying in the app carries a front end charge of up to 1.5% of what you put in, plus a PMEX charge of about 1%, shown in the app under that name. The PMEX charge is not a fee Mahaana keeps. It is the gap between the buying price and the selling price of gold on PMEX, and it is added to the fund's assets, not taken out of them. Every gold buyer in the market pays this gap. In this fund it is shown to you plainly.

### What does it cost while I hold?

The fund charges 1.5% a year for managing the gold, plus its running costs such as vault custody, takaful cover, and trustee and exchange charges. These come out of the fund's value a little each day, so the price you see in the app is already net of them. The fund's total yearly cost is published every month in the fund manager report.

### What does it cost when I sell?

The PMEX charge of about 1% applies on the way out, the same as on the way in. There is no separate exit fee in the app. The full schedule of charges is in the offering document.

### What is the PMEX charge, and why is it on the way in and on the way out?

Because that is how gold trades. On any exchange, or at any counter, the price you can sell gold for is a little lower than the price you buy it at. In this fund the two prices are published every day, so you always know exactly where you stand.

## Watching it and taking money out

### How do I see what my gold is worth?

Open the app. It shows the current value of your holding, what you put in, and how much has changed. The unit price is struck every working day at the close and published by 10.30 the next morning.

### Why did my value not change over the weekend?

Gold markets and PMEX are closed on Saturday and Sunday, so no price is struck. The value you see is Friday's close. It updates again on Monday.

### Can I add more later?

Yes, Rs 5,000 or more at a time, on any working day, at that day's price.

### How do I take money out?

Sell units in the app on any working day. The minimum you can sell at a time is Rs 5,000, or you can sell everything. The money reaches your bank account within six business days.

### Is there a lock in?

No. You can sell on any working day once the fund opens for daily dealing.

### What if everyone tries to sell at once?

On a day when sell requests add up to more than 10% of the whole fund, the requests beyond that carry over to the next working day, first come first served. This protects the investors who stay in from being forced to sell gold at a bad price.

### Can I take the gold home?

No. You buy and sell in rupees, not bars.

## Who is behind it

### Who is holding my gold?

Central Depository Company of Pakistan is the Trustee. The fund's bank accounts are in CDC's name as trustee, and the gold sits in vault custody with takaful cover paid for by the fund. None of it sits on Mahaana's books. Mahaana manages it. CDC holds it. You own it.

### Who regulates this?

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan licenses Mahaana as an asset management company and approves the fund. PMEX regulates the gold contracts the fund holds.

### Where can I read the full details?

The offering document is on our website. Please read it before you invest, in particular the investment policy, the risk factors and the taxation policies.

## Compliance flags · running list · v1

1. **IPO pricing, now per OD.** Units are issued at Rs 10 par for the whole window, per clause 1.6 and 4.4.5, because restriction xv in clause 2.6 bars investing subscription money until the IOP closes, so the announced price cannot move off par. Confirm with the dealing desk that no gold is bought before 4pm Thursday 10 September. Confirm whether bank profit earned on subscription money during the window forms part of Fund Property, which decides whether the first NAV starts a hair above Rs 10.
2. **PMEX charge at IPO.** With units at par, no spread is added to the investor's price on the way in. The market spread is paid once when the fund buys gold after the close and sits inside the first NAV. The FAQ says this plainly. Confirm compliance is comfortable with "nothing on the way in" given the spread is borne collectively through NAV.
3. **FEL waiver.** Class B units per OD 4.2 carry a front end load as specified in Annexure B. Confirm the waiver is documented in Annexure B or a term sheet, and confirm whether the waiver covers all channels or the app only. Front end load via channels other than the app can be up to 3%.
4. **Minimum redemption Rs 5,000.** The OD sets Rs 10,000 first and Rs 5,000 thereafter for investment. A minimum redemption amount was not found in the OD text. Confirm the Rs 5,000 withdrawal minimum is documented somewhere compliance will stand behind.
5. **PMEX charge naming and figure.** The app labels this the PMEX charge. The OD calls it the Price Adjustment Charge. The FAQ uses the app name only so the reader sees one term. Confirm compliance accepts the app label in promotional copy. The OD defines it as a fixed Rs 1,000 spread divided by the closing price of the deliverable gold contract, so the percentage moves with the gold price. "About 1%" is used throughout. Confirm the figure and whether it should be stated as approximate.
6. **Cut off wording.** The brief says 4pm Friday to 4pm Thursday. The OD key attributes table gives business hours as 9am to 4pm Monday to Friday. A Friday 4pm open is after the Friday cut off. Confirm the IPO window dates and times exactly as they will appear in the term sheet.
7. **Where IPO money sits.** The answer says money is held in the fund's account with the Trustee during the window. Confirm this matches the actual collection account arrangement, and whether any profit earned on it in the window belongs to the fund.
8. **Rs 100 million.** This is the SECP minimum fund size per OD 3.16, not a Mahaana target. Do not describe it as a goal or use it as a scarcity device. The FAQ frames it as a regulatory requirement.
9. **"About ten minutes" onboarding.** Carried from the homepage. Confirm.
10. **Cash allocation 2 to 5% vs OD clause 2.2 and 2.4.** The OD says at least 10% of net assets shall remain in cash and near cash instruments at all times, with gold capped at 90% of net assets on a quarterly average. A stated 2 to 5% cash holding breaches the OD's own floor. Either the answer reverts to the OD range, or the OD is amended before the fund runs at that allocation. Do not publish a figure below 10% without compliance sign off.
